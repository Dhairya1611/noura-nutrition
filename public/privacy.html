Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

What Noura stores

When you create or use an account, Noura stores your sign-in identity and the nutrition information you choose to provide. This can include your name, email address, profile image, body and fitness settings, dietary preference, calorie and macro targets, meal logs, reminder settings, streaks, and progress. If you enable closed-app reminders, Noura also stores a private browser push endpoint, delivery keys, and your device timezone.

How the information is used

Noura uses this information to restore your account across devices, calculate nutrition totals and streaks, show progress insights, schedule reminders in your timezone, and recommend possible next meals based on your selected goals.

Sign-in and cloud storage

Authentication and account data are provided through Supabase. Google sign-in supplies your basic profile, email address, and profile image when you choose that option. Noura uses row-level database rules so signed-in users can access only records associated with their own account.

Food photos, barcodes, and notifications

  • Food photos are processed on your device and are not uploaded to Noura's database.
  • If you scan or enter a barcode, the barcode value may be sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve public nutrition information.
  • After you grant permission and connect the device, Noura’s scheduled server sends meal-reminder Web Push messages to your browser’s push service. The browser can deliver them when Noura is closed. Noura does not send advertising notifications.

Local browser storage

Noura keeps a local cache in your browser to make the app responsive and support account migration. Signing out clears the local nutrition cache on that device while preserving the records stored in your account.

Control and deletion

You can sign out at any time. Signing out also unsubscribes that browser from Noura Push. The Profile screen lets you permanently delete your Noura nutrition profile, meal history, streaks, reminders, and the current device’s push subscription. Your authentication account remains available so you can sign in again.

Data sharing

Noura does not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers needed to operate the features described above, or when required by law.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Noura's features or providers change. The effective date at the top of this page will show the latest revision.

Noura is a nutrition planning tool, not a medical service. Please review the Terms of Use before relying on its estimates.
